Common Questions

Why don't you accept insurance?

Insurance reimburses shorter, lower-quality sessions and drives up overhead that gets passed to patients. Our model produces better outcomes at comparable or lower true cost. We opted out so we could actually do our jobs.

Can I use my HSA or FSA?

Yes. Physical therapy is a qualified medical expense. We accept HSA/FSA payment directly.

Do I need a physician referral?

No. Tennessee is a direct-access state. You can book an evaluation without a referral. If we find something that needs a physician's attention, we will tell you.

What if I want to use my insurance after all?

We provide a detailed superbill after each visit. Many patients successfully submit for out-of-network reimbursement and recover 40-70% of costs depending on their plan.

How many sessions will I need?

We discuss this honestly in your first visit. Most orthopedic conditions resolve in 4-8 focused sessions. We do not recommend more treatment than you need.

What is a superbill?

A superbill is a detailed receipt with the diagnosis codes, procedure codes, and provider credentials that your insurance company needs to process an out-of-network claim.
