A word from the attorney.

Meet Mr. Whitaker.

I grew up two blocks from where I practice today. The Hancock Avenue office sits across from the courthouse where my father served as a clerk for twenty-two years. He's the reason I went to law school - he saw plenty of folks come through those doors who needed counsel and couldn't find it.

I earned my JD at the University of Georgia in 1994 and opened these doors a year later. In the time since, I've raised three children with my wife Eleanor, served as president of the Bradley County Bar Association from 2018 through 2020, and built a practice on a single principle - if a matter walks into this office, it leaves with a clear answer about what to do next.

My firm is intentionally small. There is one attorney, one paralegal, and one office line. When you call, either Margaret answers or I do. When you have a question about your case, you don't go through a triage queue. You ask the attorney handling it.

I'm a member of First Presbyterian Athens, the Northeast Georgia Lions Club, and the bar associations of three surrounding counties. I treat every client like my neighbor because most of them are.

Calvin J. Whitaker, Esq.

How we work together.

From consultation to resolution.

Every matter follows the same five steps. Plain language, clear expectations, no surprises in the billing.

1

Schedule

Call or email. We'll set a one-hour consultation, in person or by phone.

2

Listen

You tell me what happened. I take notes. No legal jargon, no rushed clock.

3

Plan

Within a week, I'll give you a written outline of options, costs, and timing.

4

Represent

We move forward. You hear from me at every meaningful step, never just at billing time.

5

Close-out

Final paperwork, a closing letter, and a phone call to make sure nothing is loose.

You'll have direct access to my office line throughout your case. The number you call to schedule the consultation is the same number you call when something changes.
