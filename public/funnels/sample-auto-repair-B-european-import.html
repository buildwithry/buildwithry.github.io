What We Do

Expert Service, European Standard

Pre-Purchase Inspection

Comprehensive 90-point evaluation before you commit to a European vehicle. We uncover hidden issues, verify service history, and provide an honest assessment - because informed buyers make confident decisions.

Engine Rebuilds

Full internals machined to factory specification. From M-series straight-sixes to flat-six Porsche powerplants, our technicians rebuild with OEM-grade components and obsessive attention to tolerances.

Transmission Service

Manual, automatic, DCT, and PDK - every transmission type handled with precision. Fluid flushes, clutch replacements, and full rebuilds backed by road testing to your exact driving standard.

Electrical Diagnostics

Dealer-level diagnostic software for every European manufacturer. ISTA, XENTRY, PIWIS, ODIS - we read what the car actually says, not what generic scanners guess.

Suspension & Alignment

Factory geometry restored with four-wheel alignment to manufacturer specifications. BILSTEIN, Eibach, and OEM components. Coilover setup and corner-balancing available for track preparation.

AC Service

Refrigerant recovery, system leak testing, and full recharge. European climate systems are significantly more complex than domestic vehicles - our certified technicians handle every variant correctly.
