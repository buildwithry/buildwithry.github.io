Common Questions

GOT QUESTIONS

Do you work on all makes and models?

We specialize in Japanese and European performance platforms - Subaru, Mitsubishi, Nissan, Honda, Toyota, BMW, Porsche, and Audi. We do take select American muscle projects on a case-by-case basis. Call us if you're unsure - we'll be upfront about whether your car is a good fit for our shop.

How long does a dyno tune take?

A street tune typically runs 4-6 hours for a car in good baseline condition. A full flex-fuel or race tune can take a full day depending on how many pull cycles we need. We block the car in for a full day regardless so we're never rushing the tune. You'll get a dyno sheet and a debrief call when it's done.

Can I bring my own parts?

Yes. We'll do a receiving inspection before we install anything. If something looks wrong - a counterfeit turbo, an undersized intercooler for your power target, the wrong diameter injectors - we'll flag it and explain why before touching it. We won't install parts we don't trust.

Do you offer a dyno guarantee?

We guarantee the tune is safe and the car runs cleanly at the power level we agreed on. We don't guarantee a specific horsepower number - that depends on the hardware. What we do guarantee: no lean conditions, no knock, clean AFR across the pull, and a tune you can drive on daily without babying it.

Do you handle alignment after suspension work?

Yes - we have a Hunter alignment rack in-house and we do the alignment as part of every suspension job. If you're building a track car, we'll corner-weight it as well. We don't hand you back a car with fresh coilovers and send you down the road crooked.

What fuel do you recommend for tuned cars?

It depends on your tune. Street tunes are typically done on 93 octane and we always pull a little timing for pump-gas safety margin. If you want maximum power, we'll set you up with an E85 flex tune - it runs significantly cooler and allows for more timing advance. We'll walk you through the trade-offs at consultation.

Do you offer track day prep packages?

Yes. Our track prep package covers fluid flushes, brake inspection, tire pressure and wear check, aero setup if applicable, and a quick alignment check. We also do pre-event dyno pulls if you want confirmation everything is still in spec before hitting the track. Book at least a week out - we fill up fast around COTA and VIR weekends.
