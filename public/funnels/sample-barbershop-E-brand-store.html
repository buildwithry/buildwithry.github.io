The Space

The shop opened
in 2019.

Two chairs, a vinyl player, aged leather seats, and a straight line of sight to the street. Wicker Park was a deliberate choice - we wanted a neighborhood that took its craft seriously. The shop looks the way it sounds: unhurried, deliberate, specific.

The Barbers

Two chairs.
One standard.

Owen Park

Head Barber / Partner

Owen apprenticed under a third-generation barber in Logan Square before co-founding Blade & Co. in 2019. He cuts with scissors whenever possible - he believes clipper-only work misses half the conversation. His clients have been sitting with him for an average of four years. He's the reason they keep coming back to Wicker Park.

Book with Owen

Frankie Reyes

Senior Barber

Frankie joined the shop in 2021 after six years working in Pilsen. He trained on skin fades before branching into straight-razor shaves and beard sculpting. If you've been putting off a shave because you're not sure it's worth it - book Frankie. He'll change your view of that in about 35 minutes.
