Why commercial solar

Your roof is an underperforming asset. We fix that.

Most industrial roofs hold 80,000 sq ft of unused production capacity. We turn that surface into 25 years of predictable, hedged power - while leaving your operations untouched.

01

Zero capital, zero downtime

Power Purchase Agreement structure means we own the asset, you buy the kWh below your utility rate. Construction work happens off-hours. Roof access guaranteed.

02

One operator, twenty-five years

Same team that engineered the system services it. No EPC handoff to a third-party O&M. We carry the production warranty ourselves.

03

Battery storage when it pays

We layer 4-hour BESS only where ISO-NE capacity tags or utility demand charges make the IRR work. Storage isn't a feature - it's a financial instrument.

04

Tax-equity built in

Our partners deploy the ITC and depreciation schedule. You get the rate. We handle the IRS Form 3468, the Treasury allocation, the recapture risk.

Capabilities

Five disciplines, one development partner.

Solar generation is the entry point. Helios Solar builds the surrounding infrastructure - storage, EV, monitoring, service - so your property runs as one unified energy asset.

Rooftop & ground-mount solar

02

Solar canopies

Parking-deck and dual-use structures that deliver shade, EV charging, and rated 250+ psf snow load - without losing a single space.

03

Battery storage

4-hour BESS sized for demand-charge mitigation and ISO-NE Forward Capacity Market participation. UL 9540A tested, NFPA 855 compliant.

Operations & monitoring

How we deploy

Six steps. Roughly nine months.

Step 01 / 06
01

Site survey & production model

Drone LIDAR scan of the roof, soil resistivity for ground-mount. We model 25-year production using PVsyst with site-specific irradiance. You get a real number, not a marketing estimate.

2 weeks
02

Financial structure & PPA

We build the tax-equity stack, propose a fixed or escalator-locked PPA price, and lock interconnection capacity with the utility before the contract is signed.

3 weeks
03

Engineering & permitting

Stamped IFC drawings, structural review of the existing roof, AHJ permit submission, utility interconnection study. Helios Solar handles every signature.

8-12 weeks
04

Procurement & pre-build

Tier-1 panel and inverter procurement (we buy in advance to lock pricing), racking fabricated to your roof's exact load profile.

6-10 weeks
05

Construction & commissioning

Off-hours roof access. Single point of contact on site daily. Independent third-party commissioning agent verifies production hits guaranteed minimums.

10-16 weeks
06

Operations & long-term service

The asset enters our 24/7 SCADA. Annual production reports. Drone IR scan every six months. Same engineering team for the full 25-year term.
