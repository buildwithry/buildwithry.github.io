We don't make things pretty. We make things persuasive.

Frame & Co was founded in 2018 by Simone Varga and Leo Osei. We started with a simple conviction: most brands fail not because the product is weak, but because the story is wrong. We build the story first.

Our studio works with a small number of clients at any given time. Every engagement is run by Simone and Leo directly - no juniors on the front line, no account managers between us and the work. This is intentional.

We are headquartered in Brooklyn, NY. We work across North America and Western Europe. We don't pitch - we take on work when we believe in what the client is building.

How we work

Phase 01

Discovery

We spend the first two weeks listening. Founders, customers, competitors. We map the actual landscape, not the one in the deck.

Phase 02

Positioning

We define the one thing you should own. Not three things, not a platform - one clear, defensible position the market will remember.

Phase 03

Expression

Identity, language, and digital system. Everything that carries the position into the world, built to scale without losing its edge.

Phase 04

Activation

Launch strategy and the first 90-day playbook. We don't hand off and disappear - we stay through the moment that matters.
