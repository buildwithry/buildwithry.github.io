Our Story

Built on
Decades
of Trust.

Founded in 1982, Clearpath started as a two-man crew with a single boring machine in a rented yard. Today we operate a fleet of 14 specialized units — but the approach hasn't changed. We only take on projects we can complete on time and on budget.

Our crews average 18 years with the company. That institutional knowledge shows on every jobsite — in how we read soil conditions, how we stage equipment, and how we talk to property owners before we ever break ground.

We work with homeowners, municipalities, contractors, and Fortune 500 utilities. Every client gets the same attention to detail.
